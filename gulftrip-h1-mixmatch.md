# H1 Heading Rewrite (Variable / Mix-Match) — gulftripservice.com

Rewrite the **H1 heading** (the big visible heading on each page) for every city, cross-border route, service and tour page. Use a **variable building-block system** so no two pages share the same H1 wording — they should read naturally different, not machine-stamped from one fixed formula.

This is H1 text only. Do NOT change meta titles (already done), body content, layout, URLs, images, or pricing logic. Every H1 must remain exactly ONE per page.

## The idea
The user's base pattern is: `Private Taxi, Car Transfer and Chauffeur Service in {City}`.
Instead of stamping that identical line on every page, assemble each H1 from rotating blocks so headings vary while staying keyword-rich.

## Building blocks

**A — Service phrase (rotate; pick 2–3 services, vary order):**
- Private Taxi
- Car Transfer
- Chauffeur Service
- Airport Transfer
- Private Car Service
- Executive Car Service

**B — Connector:**
- `in {City}`
- `in {City} & Nearby Areas`
- `across {City}`
- `to & from {City}`

**C — Tail / CTA (rotate, keep short):**
- Available 24/7
- Book Anytime
- Trusted Local Drivers
- Professional & Reliable
- (sometimes no tail — a clean heading is fine too)

## Assembly rule
`{two or three A joined with commas + "&"} {B} — {C}`

Rotate the combination by page index (e.g. index mod N) so neighbouring pages differ in BOTH the service phrase and the tail. Never repeat the exact same A+B+C combo on two pages.

### City page examples (each reads differently)
- `Private Taxi, Car Transfer & Chauffeur Service in Jeddah — Available 24/7`
- `Airport Transfer & Private Car Service in Riyadh — Trusted Local Drivers`
- `Chauffeur Service, Private Taxi & Car Transfer in Dammam`
- `Executive Car Service & Airport Transfer in Makkah — Book Anytime`

### Cross-border route pages
Base: `{From} to {To}` + a service/transfer phrase + optional tail.
- `Riyadh to Dubai — Private Taxi & Cross-Border Transfer`
- `Dammam Airport to Bahrain — Chauffeur & Car Transfer Service`
- `Khafji to Kuwait Private Taxi & Border Transfer — 24/7`

### Service pages (keyword-led, no {city})
- Airport Transfers → `Airport Taxi & Private Transfer Service in Saudi Arabia`
- Hotel Transfers → `Hotel Transfer & Private Car Service — Makkah, Jeddah, Madinah`
- Private Taxi → `Private Taxi & Chauffeur Service Across Saudi Arabia`
- Umrah Transport → `Umrah Taxi & Transport — Makkah to Madinah Transfers`
- Ziyarat Services → `Ziyarat Taxi & Guided Transfer Service`

### Tour pages
- Jeddah City Tour → `Jeddah City Tour by Private Car — Half & Full Day`
- AlUla Tour → `AlUla Private Day Tour & Chauffeur Service`
- Taif Ziyarat Tour → `Taif Ziyarat Tour with Private Driver`

## Constraints
- H1 must differ from the page's meta title (don't just copy the meta title into the H1).
- Keyword (city/route/service) appears early in the H1.
- Natural English — read each one aloud; if it sounds robotic, adjust.
- No pricing, no rates, no currency.
- Exactly one H1 per page — if a page currently has more than one H1, demote the extras to H2.
- Keep H1 reasonable length (roughly under ~70 characters visible).
