## Before the tables: two things that change how Task C should be scoped

**1. A data layer already exists — this is not a greenfield Task C.**
18 of the 23 route pages already render through one shared `RoutePage` component (`src/components/RoutePage.tsx`) fed by typed objects in `src/lib/routeData.ts`. All 13 city-hub location pages already render through `CityServicePage` fed by `src/lib/cityData1/2/3.ts`. Both typed shapes (`RouteData`, `CityData`) already cover most of the brief's proposed `Route`/`Location` type in §3.2 — `origin`/`destination`/`slug`/`faqs`/`vehicleOptions` on the route side, `region`/`nearbyCities`/`airport`/`popularRoutes` on the city side. What's genuinely missing from both: an `ar` field, `tags`, `priority`, `showInNav`, and a typed `reverseSlug`/`fromSlug`/`toSlug` (today the reverse route is just a manually-written link inside `relatedRoutes`, not a structural relationship). Task C should **extend these two files**, not build parallel `data/routes.ts` / `data/locations.ts` files from scratch — two sources of truth for the same content is exactly the failure mode §0 rule 6 warns against.

**2. "Hardcoded" isn't one bucket — there are three different escape hatches from the templates, and each needs a different fix:**
- **6 pages read raw legacy HTML** via `readContentFile()` (`src/lib/readContent.ts`) from `content/*.html` files, injected with `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` — these are the `*-guide` pages, ported from a previous site and never converted to React/data. They are the longest-form content on the entire site (815–2,847 words — see Duplicates below) and cannot be edited through any template today.
- **2 route pages** (`jeddah-to-makkah-taxi-service`, `makkah-to-madinah-taxi-service`) are fully bespoke JSX that never adopted `RoutePage`, despite covering the exact same kind of content as the 18 that do.
- **A "service" bucket** (13 pages, plus the 5 cross-border hubs and 3 guide-duplicates) has no shared template at all today — every one of these 21 pages is independent JSX. This is the biggest bespoke-migration surface and isn't mentioned as its own template in §3.1; worth a `ServicePage` template in Task C alongside `RoutePage`/`LocationPage`.

## A4 — Findings

### Duplicates (9 pairs/stubs — every one listed here needs a human canonical decision, not an automated merge)

The 6 legacy-HTML `*-guide` pages are, word-for-word, the deepest content on the site — and every one of them overlaps a shorter page that a user or search engine would treat as covering the same query:

| Long-form page (words) | Overlaps with (words) | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/jeddah-airport-taxi-guide` (1,120) | `/jeddah-airport-taxi-service` (190) | same intent, 6x the depth |
| `/jeddah-to-makkah-guide` (2,378) | `/jeddah-to-makkah-taxi-service` (495) | same intent |
| `/makkah-to-madinah-guide` (1,322) | `/makkah-to-madinah-taxi-service` (86) | same intent; the "service" page is nearly empty |
| `/makkah-umrah-taxi-guide` (2,847) | `/umrah-taxi-services` (478), `/services/makkah` (298), `/ziyarat-services-in-saudi-arabia` (556) | overlaps **three** pages at once — least obvious canonical of the six |
| `/madinah-umrah-taxi-guide` (815) | `/madina-airport-taxi-service` (180), `/services/madinah` (293) | overlaps two pages |
| `/riyadh-to-dammam-guide` (818) | *(none — see below)* | not a duplicate; it's the only page covering this corridor at all |

**Recommendation:** don't auto-merge these. The long-form guide is usually the stronger page by depth, but the shorter page usually has the cleaner, more template-consistent URL and better internal linking — a human call on which URL becomes canonical (301 the other, per rule 2) is exactly what §4.2's "reject template-swap copy" review gate is for. `/riyadh-to-dammam-guide` is the exception: it's not a duplicate of anything, it's the *only* content this site has for the Riyadh↔Dammam corridor, and should be treated as the seed to migrate into `RoutePage` rather than a page to retire.

**Two more, simpler duplicates** — the noindex/orphan stub pattern:
- `/services` (124 words, noindex, zero inbound links) duplicates `/our-services` (513 words, linked from nav). Already correctly noindexed and unlinked, so it isn't actively hurting anything — but it's dead weight sitting on a real route. Recommend a 301 to `/our-services` rather than leaving a permanently-orphaned stub live.
- `/about` (309 words, noindex, orphan) duplicates `/about-us` (530 words, linked). Same situation, same recommendation: 301 to `/about-us`.
- `/contact` (53 words, noindex, orphan) duplicates `/contact-us` (232 words, linked, has an Arabic version). Same recommendation: 301 to `/contact-us`.

None of these three are slug *changes* — the old paths still resolve, just via redirect, so rule 2 is satisfied either way.

### Orphans (22 pages — zero inbound internal links from anywhere crawlable)

Pulled directly from `seo-audit/internal-link-audit.md` (regenerated fresh for this report): `/about`, `/cart`, `/checkout`, `/contact`, `/my-account`, `/pay-online`, `/services`, `/terms-conditions`, `/testimonials`, `/thank-you`, and all 12 blog posts except the index (`/blog/airport-transfer-tips`, `/blog/alula-complete-guide`, `/blog/benefits-private-airport-transfer`, `/blog/family-saudi-destinations`, `/blog/hajj-transport-guide`, `/blog/jeddah-hidden-gems`, `/blog/private-vs-rental-car`, `/blog/riyadh-to-jeddah-travel`, `/blog/safe-travel-tips-saudi`, `/blog/top-attractions-riyadh`, `/blog/umrah-travel-planning`, `/blog/vip-transport-saudi`).

Two different problems live in this one list: the noindex/utility pages (`cart`, `checkout`, `my-account`, `pay-online`, `about`, `contact`, `services`) are orphaned on purpose and that's fine. **All 12 blog posts being orphaned is not fine** — `/blog` (the index) links to none of them in the crawlable graph (its links are built from a `slug` template string the crawler can't statically resolve, though hand-checking confirms all 12 resolve correctly), and no other page links into any individual post. Every blog post on the site is reachable only by typing the URL or via `sitemap.xml`. This is a §4.3-style zero-orphan violation waiting to happen the moment CI's `seo:links` orphan check is turned on for blog content.

### Thin content

**Genuinely thin (real content pages, no data-layer excuse, under ~250 words):**
- `/makkah-to-madinah-taxi-service` — 86 words. Thinnest route page on the site, and its own `*-guide` sibling covers the same corridor at 1,322 words (see Duplicates).
- The 4 cross-border **hub** pages: `/saudi-arabia-to-qatar-taxi-service` (118w), `/saudi-arabia-to-jordan-land-transfer` (131w), `/saudi-arabia-to-uae-taxi-service` (131w), `/saudi-arabia-to-bahrain-taxi-service` (139w) — each is meant to be the aggregator/hub for several route pages underneath it and reads as a stub relative to that job.
- All **6 city-airport pages**: `/abha-airport-taxi-service` (110w — thinnest page on the entire site), `/madina-airport-taxi-service` (180w), `/taif-airport-taxi-service` (166w), `/dammam-airport-taxi-service` (187w), `/jeddah-airport-taxi-service` (190w), `/riyadh-airport-taxi-service` (205w). Worth flagging hard: the brief calls airport-transfer pages "the highest-intent page type in this vertical," and these six are thinner than every blog post on the site.

**Borderline / formulaic (all 13 city hubs, 279–352 words):** every `/services/{city}` page follows the exact same six-field shape (`whyVisit` / `challenge` / `benefit` / `popularRoutes` / `pickupPoints` / `faqs`) with the city name substituted in — the literal pattern the brief's Task A calls out ("identical to a sibling with only the city name swapped"). Not flagging these as duplicates of each other since the facts inside each field are genuinely city-specific (different airport codes, different landmark names, different distances) — but the formula is tight enough, and the word count low enough, that these are the pages most likely to read as thin/templated to a search engine, and should be the first thing enriched in Task C's copy pass, not just migrated as-is.

**Not actually thin, despite low word counts** — flagging so these aren't miscounted in Task B: `/book-online` (265w), `/checkout` (214w), `/cart` (47w), `/my-account` (131w), `/pay-online` (247w), `/faqs` (890w, not 19w — the page.tsx wrapper is thin but the real content lives in `FaqsClient.tsx`), `/contact-us` (232w, not 9w — same client-component pattern). These are transactional/functional UI, not content pages; word count isn't the right lens for them.

### Missing Arabic counterparts

**100 of 105 eligible pages have no Arabic version.** Only home, `/border-crossing`, `/contact-us`, `/dammam-airport-to-bahrain-taxi-service`, and `/dammam-airport-to-qatar-taxi-service` have one — and that list is hardcoded in `src/lib/bilingualPages.ts`, which also backs the language switcher (`arabicPathFor()`/`englishPathFor()`). That switcher already does the right thing for path-preservation (§6.7 in the header brief) for these 5 — it's the other 100 pages where switching to Arabic silently drops the visitor on the Arabic homepage instead of a 404, which is better than broken but still surprising. None of `data/routes.ts`'s proposed `ar` field exists anywhere yet; every future generated page's Arabic content has to be authored from zero, there's no partial-translation debt to inherit.

### Hardcoded pages (see also the note at the top of this document)

- **All 21 A3 service pages** — no shared template exists for this content type at all today.
- **2 route pages** bypass `RoutePage` despite fitting it exactly: `/jeddah-to-makkah-taxi-service`, `/makkah-to-madinah-taxi-service`.
- **6 guide pages** are raw legacy HTML via `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, not React/JSX at all — the deepest content on the site, in the format least able to be templated, translated, or safely edited (`content/*.html` files that don't even round-trip through TypeScript's type checker).
- **All 13 blog posts** use a shared `BlogPost` component already — not hardcoded, not a migration concern for Task C.
