# Image prompts: blog and guides, wave 1

20 November 2026. **18 images for 8 pages.**

**How to send them:**
- Generate each image from its prompt.
- Save it under the file name given. JPG or PNG is fine.
- Put it in `public/images/blog/` and tell me.

**What I do with each image:**
- convert it to WebP (768, 1280 and full size), plus a 1200×630 social image for covers;
- add the alt text below;
- place it in the section named.

**Until an image arrives:**
- A page with no cover shows an "At a glance" panel with its key figures in the hero, so nothing looks unfinished.
- In-content images simply don't appear yet.

**Rules for every image:**
- Landscape **16:9, at least 1600×900**, photographic and natural light.
- **No text, logos, number plates or watermarks.**
- **No close-up, recognisable faces.** People seen from behind or at a distance are fine.
- **Dress:** modest and local (thobes, abayas, Ihram where stated).
- **Cars:** generic sedans, SUVs and vans in white, black or silver, with no brand badges visible.
- **Not a copy of an existing photo.**
