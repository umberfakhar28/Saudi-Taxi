## Follow-up measurement

**Re-pull GSC on or after 2026-12-04** (28 days after this window closed).

Caveats for whoever reads that next report:

1. **Use a 34-day window, or normalise.** This baseline is 34 days.
2. **Four of the eight changed non-distance pages had 0–5 clicks.** A month will
   not give a clean individual read. **Judge the distance family as a block,
   on its 0.32% CTR.**
3. **Seven of the twenty changed distance pages had zero impressions.** They
   cannot show improvement; exclude them from any CTR comparison.
4. **Expect seasonality.** Umrah and school-holiday demand move independently of
   anything done here, and the window covers no major pilgrimage peak.
5. **Resolve the daily-clicks decline first.** Until the 69→16 clicks/day drop
   is explained, it will dominate any CTR signal from this work.

No ranking or traffic improvement is promised by the 2026-11-06 changes.
