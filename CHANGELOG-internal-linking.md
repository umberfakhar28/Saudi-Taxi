Branch: `seo/internal-linking`. One logical change per commit (see `git log seo/internal-linking`). Full before/after numbers in `seo-audit/post-change-report.md`; cluster design in `seo-audit/cluster-map.md`; raw findings in `seo-audit/internal-link-audit.md` / `seo-audit/link-graph.json`.

## Slugs flagged but not changed

Per the hard rule "do not change any URL or slug": `/our-services` and `/services` remain two separate, near-duplicate routes (`/services` is `noindex`); `/madina-airport-taxi-service` uses a different spelling of Madinah than every other Madinah-related route (`madinah`); the three `-guide` route pages (`/jeddah-to-makkah-guide` etc.) duplicate content already covered by their `-taxi-service` counterparts. All pre-existing, all left as-is, all noted here per the audit's own instruction.

## Not touched

Canonical tags, `robots.txt`, `noindex` flags, hreflang, main navigation structure, pricing/service-description copy, contact details, and no `rel="nofollow"` was added anywhere.
